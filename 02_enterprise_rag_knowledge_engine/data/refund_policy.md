# CloudCommerce Global Refund & Return Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase for all digital and physical products.
To initiate a return, customers must submit their receipt and order ID via the support portal.
Subscriptions cancelled within 14 days of renewal qualify for a prorated refund.
Hardware products must be returned in original packaging with all accessories.
Refund processing takes 3 to 5 business days to reflect on the original payment method.
