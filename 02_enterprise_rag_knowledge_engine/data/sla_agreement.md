# CloudCommerce Service Level Agreement (SLA)

We guarantee 99.95% monthly uptime for all Tier-1 enterprise cloud services.
Scheduled maintenance occurs on Sundays between 02:00 UTC and 04:00 UTC and is excluded from uptime calculations.
If monthly uptime drops between 99.0% and 99.95%, enterprise customers receive a 10% billing credit.
If uptime falls below 99.0%, a 25% credit is automatically applied to the subsequent invoice.
Incident response time for Severity-1 outages is strictly guaranteed within 15 minutes.
